Common questions

How do I cancel a booking?

Open Bookings → tap your booking → Cancel this booking. You can also cancel from Live Tracking when available. Paid bookings are refunded automatically when possible via Stripe. New bookings are limited to a 30-day window.

Payment or refund questions?

We use Stripe for checkout. A 20% platform fee is shown in the booking summary and on provider earnings screens (providers keep 80% of the service base; tips go 100% to the provider). Sparkefy does not store full card details.

Live tracking not working?

Make sure location permission is enabled for Sparkefy. Tracking is available on supported jobs within the 30-day booking window. If it still fails, email us with your booking ID and device model.

How do I become a provider or edit my showcase?

In Profile, choose Become a Service Provider, then use Edit My Showcase & Pricing to add cover photos, work samples, and price estimates.

Report a problem with a pro or customer?

Email us with details and the booking ID. We review all reports and may follow up for more information.

Sign in with Apple or account issues?

Try signing out and back in. If you used Sign in with Apple and hid your email, reply from the private relay address you registered with, or tell us the name and ZIP on the account. We can help restore access when possible.

Still need help?

Just email sparkefysupport@gmail.com — we’re happy to assist.
